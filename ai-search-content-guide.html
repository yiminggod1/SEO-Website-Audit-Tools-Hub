GUIDE • AI SEARCH

Make information easy to understand before making it easy to summarize.

There is no universal “AI SEO score” that guarantees visibility. A stronger approach is to improve the underlying information: direct answers, evidence, clear entities and navigable structure.

Advertisement
01

Answer the question early

Put the page’s primary conclusion or definition where a reader can find it quickly. Do not make every important answer depend on several paragraphs of setup.

02

Support important claims

Use concrete examples, sources, measurements and definitions. Unsupported assertions are harder for people—and downstream systems—to evaluate.

03

Keep entities consistent

Use the same names for products, organizations, people and concepts across headings and related pages so relationships remain understandable.

04

Create topical connections

Link related pages with useful context instead of building isolated keyword pages. A guide should naturally lead into the specialist tool that helps the reader act.

05

Keep the promise narrow

AI-assisted search systems change. Treat these practices as information-quality improvements, not a guarantee of inclusion, citation or rankings.
