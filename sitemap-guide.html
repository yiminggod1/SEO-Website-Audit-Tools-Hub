GUIDE • DISCOVERY

Build a sitemap that reflects the site you actually want discovered.

A sitemap is strongest when it reinforces your canonical site architecture instead of becoming a dump of every URL the CMS has ever produced.

Advertisement
01

Include preferred URLs

Start with canonical, indexable pages that matter to users. Avoid filling the sitemap with duplicates, tracking variants or URLs you would not recommend to a visitor.

02

Keep it current

Update the sitemap as content is added or retired. Broken or stale entries reduce its usefulness as a discovery aid.

03

Match the site

A sitemap should broadly agree with your internal linking and canonical choices. Large differences are worth investigating rather than ignoring.

04

Use the robots reference

Expose the sitemap location in robots.txt so crawlers can discover the file even when they do not start at your homepage.

05

Use the generator carefully

The SEO Audit Hub sitemap generator accepts one URL per line and produces a simple XML structure. Review the final list before deployment.

Use the related tools
