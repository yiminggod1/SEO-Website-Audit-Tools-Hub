HOW IT WORKS

Use the tool as a diagnostic, not a magic score.

Results are deliberately explained so you can decide what to change and what not to change.

01

Inventory links

Count linked elements with href attributes and classify them by their URL shape.

02

Find weak anchors

Flag unnamed links so visually or programmatically important navigation is easier to audit.

03

Plan the next click

Use the output to review topical relationships, navigation depth and whether important pages receive contextual links.

SEARCH INTENT

Internal links are part of information architecture.

A high count is not automatically good. The useful question is whether links help people reach related, important pages without creating noise.

For production crawls, combine this markup-level inspection with server-side URL discovery and HTTP status checks.
