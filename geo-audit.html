HOW IT WORKS

Use the tool as a diagnostic, not a magic score.

Results are deliberately explained so you can decide what to change and what not to change.

01

State the answer

Put the page’s primary answer or outcome close to the relevant question instead of burying it.

02

Support claims

Use specific facts, sources, examples and definitions so important statements can be evaluated.

03

Clarify entities

Use consistent names, relationships, headings and page context so the subject is unambiguous.

SEARCH INTENT

GEO is not a secret ranking score.

AI-assisted search systems can summarize content in different ways, and no checklist guarantees inclusion. The practical win is making your information clearer, better supported and easier to navigate.

That same discipline improves human usability, too.
